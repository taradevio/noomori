Stovetop Lentils
Servings: 3

Ingredients
- 1 cup lentils
- 3 cup water
- 1/2 tsp salt

Instructions
1. Rinse the lentils under cool water.
Drain them well before adding them to the pan.
2. Add the lentils and water to a saucepan.
Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer gently.
3. Stir in the salt near the end of cooking.
