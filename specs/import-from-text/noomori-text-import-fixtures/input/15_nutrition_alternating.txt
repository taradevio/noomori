Ingredients
- 2 cup cooked chickpeas
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 cup chopped vegetables

Instructions
1. Warm the chickpeas in a pan.
2. Add the vegetables and cook briefly.
3. Finish with olive oil and serve.
