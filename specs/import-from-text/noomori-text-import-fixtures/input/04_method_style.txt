Creamy Pea Pasta
Prep 10 mins
Cook 18 mins
Serves 4

Ingredients
* 300g pasta
* 150g frozen peas
* 2 tbsp grated cheese
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 1 small bunch parsley, chopped

Method
1. Cook the pasta until just tender.
2. Add the peas for the final few minutes.
3. Drain, then toss with cheese, oil, and parsley.
