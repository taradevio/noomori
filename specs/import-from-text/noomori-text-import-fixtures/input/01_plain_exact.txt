Weeknight Lemon Rice
A quick rice dish with lemon and herbs.

Servings: 4
Prep Time: 10 min
Cook Time: 20 min

Ingredients
- 2 cup cooked rice
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 clove garlic
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp lemon juice

Instructions
1. Warm the olive oil in a pan.
2. Add the garlic and cook briefly.
3. Stir in the rice, salt, and lemon juice.
4. Cook until hot and serve.
