# Roasted Tomato Couscous

A warm couscous bowl with roasted tomatoes.

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Servings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 min | 22 min | 3 |

## Ingredients
- 1 cup couscous
- 250 g cherry tomatoes
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Instructions
1. Roast the tomatoes until softened.
2. Prepare the couscous according to the package.
3. Fold the tomatoes into the couscous and serve.
