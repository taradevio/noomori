Pan-Fried Corn Cakes
Servings: 4

Ingredients
- 1 cup corn kernels
- 1 egg
- 1/2 cup flour

Instructions
1. Mix the corn, egg, and flour.
2. Spoon into a hot pan.
3. Cook until browned on both sides.

Key Notes
- Keep the batter thick enough to hold its shape.
- Serve immediately for the crispest texture.
