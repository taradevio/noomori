Citrus Herb Chicken
Bright pan-cooked chicken for a simple dinner.
Recipe Summary
Prep Time
	Cook Time
	Servings
	Difficulty
	12 minutes
	28 minutes
	4
	Easy

Ingredients
For the Chicken
* 4 piece chicken thighs
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 1 tsp salt
For the Finish
* 2 tbsp lemon juice
* 1 tbsp chopped parsley

Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Pat the chicken dry and season it.
2. Heat the oil and brown the chicken.
3. Lower the heat and cook until done.
4. Add lemon juice and parsley before serving.
