# Authentic Turkish Köfte (Turkish Meatballs)

## Recipe Overview

Köfte is a beloved Turkish classic—juicy, ground meat patties packed with warm spices, onion, garlic, and fresh herbs. They can be grilled, pan-seared, or baked, and are traditionally served with sumac onion salad, warm flatbread, and garlic yogurt or cacık.

## Ingredients

### For the Köfte:

- **500g (1.1 lbs)** ground lamb or beef (or a 50/50 mix, 80/20 fat content preferred)
- **1 medium** yellow onion, finely grated
- **2 cloves** garlic, minced or grated
- **½ cup** breadcrumbs (or panko/panko​ sub)
- **1 large** egg
- **¼ cup** fresh parsley, finely chopped
- **1 tsp** ground cumin
- **1 tsp** ground coriander
- **1 tsp** Turkish red pepper flakes (_Pul Biber_ / Aleppo pepper) or sweet paprika
- **1 tsp** dried oregano
- **1 tsp** salt
- **½ tsp** freshly cracked black pepper
- **1 tbsp** olive oil (for cooking)

### For Serving (Optional but Recommended):

- Warm pita or lavash bread
- **Sumac Onion Salad:** Thinly sliced red onions tossed with sumac, fresh parsley, lemon juice, and olive oil
- **Garlic Yogurt / Cacık:** Plain Greek yogurt mixed with grated cucumber, minced garlic, dried mint, and salt

---

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the Onion:** Grate the yellow onion using a box grater. Squeeze the grated onion over a sink using your hands or a fine-mesh strainer to extract excess liquid. _(Discard the juice; this keeps the köfte firm and prevents them from falling apart)._
2. **Mix the Köfte:** In a large mixing bowl, combine the drained grated onion, ground meat, minced garlic, breadcrumbs, egg, chopped parsley, cumin, coriander, Pul Biber, oregano, salt, and black pepper.
3. **Knead Thoroughly:** Knead the mixture firmly with your hands for about 5 minutes until it becomes cohesive, sticky, and uniform in texture.
4. **Rest (Optional but Best):** Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes (up to 4 hours) to allow the meat to absorb all the spice flavors.
5. **Shape the Patties:** Divide the mixture into 12–16 equal portions. Roll each portion into a ball, then flatten into an oval or round patty roughly ½-inch thick.
6. **Cook the Köfte:** Heat olive oil in a large skillet or grill pan over medium-high heat. Add the köfte in a single layer without overcrowding. Fry for 3–4 minutes per side, turning once, until nicely browned on the outside and cooked through (internal temp of 160°F / 71°C).
7. **Serve:** Garnish with fresh parsley and serve hot alongside sumac onion salad, warm flatbread, and garlic yogurt sauce.

---

## Recipe Notes

- **Meat Selection:** In Turkey, lamb or a beef/lamb blend is standard and delivers authentic flavor. If using beef alone, ensure it has at least 20% fat to keep the patties moist.
- **Spice Adjustment:** Pul Biber (Aleppo pepper) gives a mild, fruity heat. If you prefer extra spice, add a pinch of cayenne or extra red pepper flakes.
- **Freezing:** Shaped raw köfte patties can be frozen on a baking sheet, then transferred to a freezer bag for up to 3 months. Thaw completely in the fridge before cooking.
