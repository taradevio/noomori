Mild Indian Goat Curry

This authentic recipe has a deep, rich flavour.

INGREDIENTS

  • 500g diced goat meat
  • 2 rounded tablespoons ground coriander
  • 2 large onions, chopped
  • 150ml water

METHOD

  1. Mix the spices with the garlic and salt.
  2. Fry the onions until golden brown.
  3. Add the meat and bring to the boil.

Serve with tatties or rice.
