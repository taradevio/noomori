Pumpkin Bread

Ingredients

For the pumpkin bread:

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon nutmeg
  • 1/2 teaspoon allspice
  • 1 cup pumpkin puree
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 2 teaspoons molasses
  • 1 teaspoon orange zest
  • 1/2 cup chopped pecans

For the orange glaze (optional):

  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 tablespoons orange juice
  • 1 teaspoon orange zest

Directions

  1. Make the homemade pumpkin purée (optional):

    Cut and roast the pumpkin.

    Cool it, then scoop out the flesh.

  2. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C):

    Butter a loaf pan.

  3. Whisk the dry ingredients

    Whisk together the flour, salt, baking soda, and spices.

  4. Combine the wet ingredients:

    Mix together the pumpkin, sugar, butter, eggs, water, and molasses.

  5. Make the batter:

    Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients.

    Do not overmix.

  6. Bake:

    Pour the batter into the pan and bake until done.

  7. Remove from pan and cool completely:

    Cool briefly in the pan.

    Transfer the loaf to a rack.

  8. Glaze:

    Whisk together the glaze ingredients.

    Drizzle over the cooled loaf.

  9. Enjoy!
