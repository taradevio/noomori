Chicken Pot Pie With Buttermilk Biscuit Topping

Unrelated recipe introduction.

Prep Time: 20 mins

Ingredients

For the Chicken:

  • 2 quarts (1.9 L) chicken stock
  • 4 1/2 pounds (2 kg) chicken pieces
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 2 large carrots, diced
  • 2 celery ribs, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 sprigs thyme
  • 1 sprig parsley
  • 1 sprig rosemary
  • 1 bay leaf

For the Filling:

  • 1/2 ounce gelatin
  • 1/4 cup reserved stock
  • 4 ounces unsalted butter
  • 4 ounces all-purpose flour
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 1 large carrot, diced
  • 1 celery rib, diced
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine
  • 1 quart reserved stock
  • 5 ounces frozen peas
  • 3 ounces diced pimentos
  • 1 3/4 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 1/4 ounce thyme leaves
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
  • 28 ounces poached chicken
Advertisement inside ingredients

For the Biscuit Topping:

  • 9 ounces all-purpose flour
  • 1 ounce sugar
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 8 ounces cold unsalted butter
  • 8 ounces cultured buttermilk

Directions

  1. For the Chicken: Combine the chicken, stock, vegetables, and herbs.

  2. Poach the chicken gently, then strain and reserve the stock.

  3. For the Filling: Bloom the gelatin and prepare a blond roux.

  4. Whisk in wine and stock, then add the vegetables and seasoning.

  5. Shred the chicken and divide the filling among baking dishes.

  6. For Biscuit Topping: Mix the dry ingredients, butter, and buttermilk.

  7. Bake until the biscuits are golden and the filling bubbles.

Special Equipment

Large pot and baking dish.
