Macaroni Salad

8 servings 15 minutes 10 minutes

Ingredients US CustomaryMetric

  • 8 ounces macaroni
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs

Dressing

  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard

Instructions

  1. Cook the macaroni, then drain and cool it.
  2. Mix the dressing and fold it into the macaroni.

Notes

  • Keep refrigerated after serving.
  • Mix only until combined.
